Brand Identity Guide

A "High-Growth" framework for professional authority and educational innovation.

Color Palette

Smart Green #01963A
Saffron Gold #D4AF37
Warm Cream #F4F1EA
Soft Charcoal #2D2D2D

Typography Strategy

Montserrat (Headers)

Used for titles and high-impact statements. Montserrat communicates a geometric, modern, and reliable presence. It is bold enough to command attention in a CV or presentation while remaining professional.

Inter (Body Text)

Used for all long-form content. Inter is designed specifically for computer screens, ensuring maximum readability for digital portfolios and recruitment platforms.

Design Logic

This identity rejects the standard "Corporate Blue" in favor of a palette that feels organic yet high-status. It is tailored for an American professional operating in a high-growth environment.

  • The Foundation: Warm Cream serves as the primary background color. It is less harsh than pure white and feels more sophisticated and "premium."
  • The Driver: Smart Green represents vitality, growth, and stability. Use this for primary call-to-action buttons, icons, or highlighting key career achievements.
  • The Prestige: Saffron Gold is used sparingly for accents. It signals excellence and high-value experience (the "Gold Standard"). Use it for dividers or to emphasize specific data points.
  • The Authority: Soft Charcoal provides the grounding. It ensures text is legible without the high contrast of absolute black, which can feel dated.

Usage Guidelines

Keep the layouts clean. Use Smart Green for your name and section headers, and keep the Soft Charcoal for the bulk of your descriptive text. Ensure at least 60% of any design uses the Warm Cream to maintain a sense of space and modernism.
